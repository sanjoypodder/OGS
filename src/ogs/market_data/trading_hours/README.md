# TradingHours Module

The **TradingHours** module provides standardized trading schedules for
markets, exchanges, and financial instruments within the OGS Smart Money AI
framework.

It defines when trading sessions are open or closed, enabling accurate
market-state determination for analytics, Smart Money concepts, and automated
trading workflows.

Typical use cases include:

- Exchange trading schedules
- Market opening and closing times
- Pre-market sessions
- Regular trading sessions
- Post-market sessions
- Overnight trading sessions
- Extended trading hours
- Special trading sessions

---
